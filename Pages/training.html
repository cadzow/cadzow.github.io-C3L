Training delivery

Notwithstanding any of the caveats regarding standards and development we feel (that is Scott feels) it is important to make sure there are experts in the field and we aim to ensure that training in the core technologies of cybersecurity is available to those wishing to develop their expertise. In some cases we will simply direct you to other providers if we feel their material is better suited to you but we will also offer a set of pre-defined courses on set dates and locations. These will run at normal commercial rates (we're not going to undercut the competition but rather offer the expertise you need at a competitive rate) based on the needs of our clients, the number of participants, the location and so on.

In addition to any training courses developed in-house we will partner with appropriate parties. This means with people such as with WrayCastle course providers to deliver training in the field of cybersecurity. We will also work with other parties to deliver training in the field of standardisation and security.

Training development

There will be times where the off the shelf courses from ourselves and the wider market simply fail to meet your requirements. in such cases we will work with you and other partners to develop a bespoke, or near bespoke, course. This will be more expensive than an existing course but that additional cost is offset against the tailored fit.

Sponsorship as engagement

In times gone by artists and artisans often had their work supported by simple patronship. In sport and arts today we see that role being taken by sponsors. So when a brand sponsors the league cup or a formula 1 team it doesn't mean that they are directly marketing the football team or the car but win by association to a winner. In recognsing that C3L is best of breed and that association with the best is a good thing I'm going to make the relatively unusual step of explicitly listing sponsorship as a means of maintaining C3L at the forefront of standards development and security work. The suggestions listed below are for 4 levels of sponsorship (Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum) with increasingly interactive relationships between C3L and our patrons or sponsors as the levels go up.

Sponsorship, or patronship, is not a trivial undertaking and the levels suggested are not insignificant. However even the simplest of standards can absorb years of effort, attending meetings, building consensus and of course writing, testing, validating to ensure the resultant standard is fit for purpose. In light of this every sponsor will be acknowledged on this website and on the documentation produced by the company with the assistance of their sponsorship, which could be contributions to standards or to the marketing material of the company. In all cases the wishes of the sponsor for visibility will be respected. The forms of visibility of the sponsor may include:

  • have your name or company logo incorporated on any publicity material produced
  • joint involvement in standards tutorials, seminars, conferences and so forth

The following list suggests what a partonship or sponsorship opportunity could lead to.

  • BRONZE £12,500 per annum excluding VAT
    • This would cover 1 major standards meeting by a work item rapporteur
    • Work supported would be development of contributions, presentation and follow up to development of the work item itself.
  • SILVER £25,000 per annum excluding VAT
    • Enhanced support to standards
    • Extends Bronze with on-site standards development training and advice on involving the organization in standardisation
  • GOLD £50,000 per annum excluding VAT
    • Standards development support
    • Extends Silver level with critical editorial and technical review of all standards development contributions made by the sponsor
  • PLATINUM £100,000 per annum excluding VAT
    • Co-operative standards development
    • Extends Gold to include on site support and collaboration on development of contributions, and on the development of a standards development strategy
